# When the live record can say something

**Date:** 25 Nov 2026
**Status:** written before those match counts exist. The settled ledger has 10 matches today.

Two looks are fixed now. They are the only times a range on the live record is allowed to be read as a check on the model.

| Settled matches | What the look is for |
|---|---|
| 380 | One season. Ask whether the live record is in the same region as the backtest. On 380 matches, a change of about 0.02 nats is still too small to confirm. |
| 1,140 | Three seasons. This is the first look large enough to speak to the backtest gap on home, draw or away: Dixon-Coles minus the closing book is 0.0338 (95% range 0.0128 to 0.0606, 1,430 matches). |

Between those two counts, every range on the site is labelled descriptive. A descriptive range shows how much the average has moved. It is not a stopping rule, and it is not a reason to change a setting, refit a weight, or declare a winner.

Ten matches, one round, cannot show skill. The review's uncertainty on a 10-match average gap is about 0.3 nats. The backtest gap on home, draw or away is 0.0338 (95% range 0.0128 to 0.0606, 1,430 matches). The week is listed so the record is complete.

The bookmaker is the competitor. A smaller log loss than the book on a short record is not a win over the book. No tile shows a streak, a return, or a last-10 summary.
